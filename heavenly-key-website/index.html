Message from the President

Welcome to Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement.

Our burden is to see a generation revived, set apart for holiness, and fully prepared for the second coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. We are called to take Christ to the nations, to raise prayerful believers, and to win souls for His Kingdom.

I invite you to be part of this global mission through prayer, partnership and active involvement. Together, we can see lives transformed and nations reached for Christ.

Yours in Christ,
