Steps to Salvation

Salvation is God's gift of grace through faith in Jesus Christ. It is not earned by church membership or good works.

01

Recognize Your Need

All have sinned and need God's mercy and forgiveness.

Romans 3:23
02

Believe in Jesus Christ

Believe that Jesus Christ died for our sins and rose again.

Romans 10:9–10
03

Repent and Turn to God

Turn away from sin and sincerely surrender your life to Christ.

Acts 3:19
04

Receive Christ by Faith

Call upon the Lord Jesus and trust Him as your Saviour and Lord.

Romans 10:13
05

Follow Jesus Daily

Grow through Scripture, prayer, obedience, holiness and fellowship with believers.

2 Peter 3:18

A Prayer of Surrender

You may speak to God sincerely in your own words. This example can help you express your faith and repentance; the words themselves are not what saves you.

Lord Jesus, I acknowledge that I am a sinner and need Your forgiveness. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I repent and turn to You. Please forgive me, make me new, and help me follow You in holiness and truth. I receive You as my Saviour and Lord. Amen.

If you have decided to follow Christ, begin reading the Bible, praying daily and connecting with a Bible-believing Christian fellowship.
