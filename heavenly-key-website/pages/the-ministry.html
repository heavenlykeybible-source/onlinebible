About the Movement

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement is an End-Time, Non-Denominational, Apostolic and Evangelistic Movement committed to preaching the undiluted Word of God, uniting the Body of Christ and upholding the faith once delivered to the saints.

We work with Bible-believing ministers, ministries and believers to reach the unreached, organize crusades and revival programmes, distribute Gospel materials and raise a network of believers devoted to Christ and the Great Commission.

The Great Commission

Evangelism is central to our calling. We answer Christ’s command to go into all the world, make disciples and be witnesses of Him to the uttermost parts of the earth.

Our Mission

To rescue the perishing from the bondage of sin, Satan and hell through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Vision

  1. Bring sinners to Christ and equip the saints to be ready for the coming of the Lord.
  2. Raise an End-Time Army of Christ: apostles, pastors, prophets, evangelists, teachers, Kingdom financiers and sound believers.
  3. Take Christ to the nations through crusades, revival programmes and evangelistic outreach.
  4. Spread revival, holiness and the power of God across churches, communities and nations.
  5. Unite the Body of Christ and uphold the faith of our fathers.

Our Motto

CHRIST FOR ALL NATIONS
