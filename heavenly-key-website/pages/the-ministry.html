The Ministry

Taking Christ to the nations of the world

Our mission is to rescue the perishing through the Gospel of Jesus Christ and equip believers for faithful Christian living.

We work with Bible-believing ministers, ministries and churches to organize crusades, revival programmes, outreach and the distribution of Gospel materials.

Our vision is to raise an End-Time Army of Christ and spread revival, holiness and the power of God.

WHO WE ARE

Our Story

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement is an End-time, Non-denominational, Apostolic, and Evangelistic Movement, preaching the undiluted word of God; earnestly contending for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints (Jude 3).

Partnering with Bible-believing ministers and ministries to preach the word of God to the unreached, we are a network of the End-time Army of Christ — men and women who have had a genuine encounter of salvation with Jesus Christ, from different Bible-believing churches — organizing crusades and revival programs across churches, communities and nations of the world, and distributing free Gospel materials: Bibles, CDs, magazines, and more.

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement serves the wider Body of Christ as a non-denominational evangelistic and apostolic network. We desire the progress, unity and spiritual strength of Bible-believing Christians across denominations; wherever Christ is truly honoured and His Word upheld, we seek fellowship and cooperation for the Gospel.

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement welcomes anyone who has been saved with a burden to rescue the perishing to partner with us for the good of the Gospel. The coming of our Lord and Saviour Jesus is at hand and nearer than ever, and many are unreached and unsaved — therefore come with us.
