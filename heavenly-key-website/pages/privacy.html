Privacy

Privacy Policy

How personal information is handled across the ministry website and Global Christian Community.

What we collect

Depending on how you use the services, we may process account and profile information, contact details, ministry enquiries, prayer or testimony submissions, community posts and messages, moderation reports and decisions, social sign-in identifiers, administrator audit records, and basic technical information needed for security and operation.

Why we use it

We use information to provide ministry and community features, authenticate accounts, protect users, respond to requests, moderate content, operate broadcasts and connected services, maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with lawful obligations.

Sensitive information

Prayer, counselling, testimony and religious information can be sensitive. Share only what is necessary. Confidential ministry inboxes and restricted administrative records are limited by role-based access, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Cookies and sessions

Authenticated areas use secure session cookies. They are used to keep you signed in and protect account access, not to create a public advertising profile.

Service providers and connected platforms

Cloudflare infrastructure is used for hosting, databases, storage and network protection. Optional social sign-in or publishing features may connect to providers such as Google, Meta, TikTok, X or LinkedIn when an authorized administrator or user chooses to connect them. Those providers apply their own privacy terms.

Media and retention

Temporary ministry media can be configured for automatic expiry; the current media architecture normally uses a 48-hour temporary retention window unless an authorized administrator marks an item to be kept. Other records may be retained for operational, safety, audit, dispute-resolution or lawful reasons appropriate to their purpose.

Your choices and requests

Global Community members can use Safety & privacy to request access, correction, deletion or review. Other privacy questions can be sent through the ministry contact page. We may need to verify a request before acting on it and may retain information where a legitimate safety, security, legal or record-keeping reason requires it.

Children and the Global Community

The public ministry website may be viewed generally, but new Global Christian Community accounts are currently intended for adults aged 18 or older and use an age self-attestation during registration.

Lawful requests

Government or law-enforcement requests do not bypass authentication or grant direct account access. Requests are reviewed for lawful authority, scope and authenticity. Questions about lawful requests can be directed through the contact page.

Online Assistant and automated processing

Messages sent to our Online Assistant may be processed by ministry technology services and configured AI providers to generate responses and maintain safety. Please do not submit passwords, payment card information or confidential identity documents. AI responses may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not a substitute for professional advice.

International processing and privacy rights

Hosting and connected services may process information outside Nigeria. We seek to handle such processing in accordance with applicable data protection requirements. Subject to applicable law, individuals may request access, correction, deletion or review of their personal information through the ministry contact channels.

How information is collected

Information may be provided directly when you register, submit forms, contact the ministry, request prayer or participate in community features. Limited technical and usage information may also be generated automatically when you access the website or connected services.

Access, confidentiality and security

Access to personal records should be limited to authorized personnel who need the information for their assigned responsibilities. We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including account access controls and operational security measures. No online system can guarantee that unauthorized access will never occur.

Processing grounds and consent

Depending on the activity and applicable law, information may be processed to provide requested services, administer accounts, fulfil legal obligations, protect users and the platform, or on the basis of consent where required. Where processing relies on consent, you may request to withdraw it, subject to lawful exceptions.

Data retention and deletion

We seek to retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, subject to security, legal, operational and record-keeping requirements. You may request deletion or correction using the ministry's contact channels. Some information may need to be retained where applicable law or a legitimate dispute or security requirement permits it.

Complaints and privacy questions

For access, correction, deletion, objection or other privacy requests, contact contact@heavenlykey.org. You may also have the right to complain to the relevant data protection authority under applicable law.

Contact

Email contact@heavenlykey.org or use the contact page. Do not send passwords, unnecessary identity documents, or copies of illegal material.

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This notice describes the platform as currently built and should be reviewed when jurisdictions, providers or processing activities materially change.
