Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement

Statement of Faith

The Bible is our final authority for faith, doctrine and Christian living.

The Holy Bible

We believe the Old and New Testaments are the inspired Word of God and the final authority for Christian faith, conduct and ministry.

The Godhead

We believe in the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost, distinct and perfectly united in the Godhead.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the virgin birth of Jesus Christ, His sinless life, crucifixion, death, burial, bodily resurrection and His coming again.

Sin, Repentance & Salvation

Humanity is sinful and in need of redemption. We believe in genuine repentance, faith in Jesus Christ and salvation by the grace of God, producing a transformed and obedient life.

Restitution & Holy Living

We believe believers should make right the wrongs they can properly correct, maintain a clear conscience, and live a consecrated life of holiness and obedience to God.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the baptism and present work of the Holy Spirit, His power for Christian witness, and the gifts of the Spirit according to Scripture.

Healing & Redemption

We believe in God’s redemptive provision through Jesus Christ and in prayer for divine healing, deliverance and wholeness according to His Word.

Personal Evangelism

We believe every Christian is called to be a compassionate witness for Christ and to participate in the Great Commission.

End-Time Hope

We believe the prophetic promises of Scripture concerning the return of Jesus Christ and the responsibility of believers to remain watchful, faithful and prepared.
