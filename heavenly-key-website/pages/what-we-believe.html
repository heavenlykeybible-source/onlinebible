Statement of Faith

The Bible is our final authority

We believe the Old and New Testaments are the inspired Word of God and the final authority for Christian faith, conduct and ministry.

We believe in God the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost; in the virgin birth, sinless life, death, burial, bodily resurrection and return of Jesus Christ.

We believe in repentance, salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, holy living, the present work of the Holy Spirit, prayer, divine healing and personal evangelism.
