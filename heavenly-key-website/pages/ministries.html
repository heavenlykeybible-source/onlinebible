Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement

Our Ministries

Experience the life of Jesus through the many different ministries at Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement.

  • Ezekiel 22:30 – So I sought for a man among them who would make a wall, and stand in the gap before Me on behalf of the land…
  • John 4:34 – Jesus saith unto them, My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work.
  • Hebrews 10:7 – Then said I, Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of me,) to do thy will, O God.

We have come to live and die for what we believe, for the divine mandate and assignment we were sent to fulfil on planet earth. Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement has a specific mandate to stand in the gap and provide spiritual covering for the Church and the Kingdom of God.

SACRIFICIALLY GLORIFYING GOD AND HELPING HUMANITY!

At Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement, our goal, purpose, vision, and aim are to faithfully serve God, glorify Jesus, and help humanity. We believe that a fulfilled life is all about truly serving God, truly helping humanity, and ultimately making it to heaven at last.

We Minister in a Variety of Ways

1. Internet Ministry

Taking Bible truth, revival messages and evangelistic outreach across digital platforms.

2. Radio / Television

ONLINE REVIVAL RADIO and FAITH OF OUR FATHERS TV for preaching, prayer, discipleship and worldwide Gospel access.

3. Gospel Crusade

Crusades and revival programmes across churches, communities and nations.

4. Van / Motor Campaign Ministry

Mobile evangelism that carries the Gospel into streets, markets and communities.

5. Charity Ministry

Serving humanity with compassion while pointing people to Jesus Christ.

6. Print Media Ministry

Distributing Gospel materials, Bible teaching, tracts and other Christian resources.

7. School Outreach

Reaching students and young people with salvation, holiness, purpose and Christian discipleship.

8. Global Minister's Program (GMP)

Encouraging and networking Bible-believing ministers for revival and the Great Commission.

9. Hospital Visitation

Prayer, encouragement and compassionate ministry to the sick and their families.

10. Prison Visitation

Taking the hope and transforming Gospel of Jesus Christ to people in correctional facilities.

11. Bible School

Equipping believers and workers with sound Bible teaching for Christian life, ministry and evangelism.
