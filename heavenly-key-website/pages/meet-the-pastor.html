Meet the President

A burden for revival, holiness and the nations

Pastoring the Nations

Evangelist Emmanuel Ogheneochuko Adiohwo is an end-time non-denominational pastor, evangelist and teacher with a burden to reach people with the Gospel of Jesus Christ through evangelistic meetings, revival programmes and media.

His theological training and years of studying evangelistic ministry have helped prepare him for the Movement’s calling to reach the nations through the power of the Holy Spirit.

His ministry emphasis includes salvation, holiness, prayer, fasting, the Word of God, healing, deliverance, revival and equipping believers to discover and fulfil their God-given purpose.

He carries a burden to see people saved, sanctified, healed, delivered and prepared for the coming of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Evangelist Emmanuel Ogheneochuko Adiohwo

President & Founder — Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement

Pastoring the Nations!

Emmanuel Ogheneochuko Adiohwo is a passionate End-time Non-denominational Pastor, Evangelist, and Teacher, reaching thousands with the good news of Jesus Christ through Gospel meetings and online media across Nigeria, Africa, and beyond.

He studied Theology at Deeper Life Bible School (IBTC — International Bible Training College), and has studied intensively the evangelistic ministries of Evangelist Reinhard Bonnke, British Evangelist Smith Wigglesworth, Evangelist Joseph Ayo Babalola, Benson Andrew Idahosa, Pastor W.F. Kumuyi, and Pastor Benny Hinn.

His spiritual mentors include Apostle Joseph Ayo Babalola, Arch-Bishop Benson Idahosa, Evangelist Reinhard Bonnke, Pastor W.F. Kumuyi, and Pastor Benny Hinn.

He preaches the Gospel in the power of the Holy Spirit, and has a mandate and passion to train and equip the Body of Christ to discover, develop, and fulfil God-given purpose.
