About Us

A global movement for revival, holiness and evangelism

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement is an End-Time, Non-Denominational, Apostolic and Evangelistic Movement taking Christ to the nations of the world.

We unite Bible-believing Christians, proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ and prepare people for His coming.

Our calling is to preach Christ, uphold the faith of our fathers, strengthen believers and reach communities through evangelism and prayer.
