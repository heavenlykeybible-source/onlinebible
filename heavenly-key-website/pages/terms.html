Terms & Community Standards

Rules for respectful, lawful and Christ-centered use of Heavenly Key services.

Purpose

Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement provides ministry information, Christian fellowship, prayer, discipleship, broadcasts and related community features. Use the services in a manner consistent with law, safety, respect for others and the Christ-centered purpose of the platform.

Accounts

You are responsible for information submitted through your account and for protecting your credentials. Do not impersonate another person, share access improperly, interfere with security controls or attempt unauthorized access. Global Christian Community registration is currently for adults aged 18 or older.

Prohibited conduct and content

Do not use the services for sexual exploitation or child abuse material, credible threats, hate or targeted harassment, fraud, scams, impersonation, doxxing, unlawful trade, malicious software, unauthorized surveillance, infringement of others’ rights, or other unlawful activity. Do not upload media you lack the right to use.

Moderation

Content may be reported, held, limited or removed after review. Automated text checks are only review aids and are not treated as proof of wrongdoing. Serious account restrictions require authorized human review. Members can use the Safety & privacy process to request review or appeal supported decisions.

Privacy and messages

Use privacy controls carefully. Community messages are not represented as end-to-end encrypted. A reported message may be reviewed by authorized staff for safety and moderation. See the Privacy Notice.

Broadcasts and third-party platforms

Availability of radio, television, live video and social publishing can depend on network conditions and third-party services. Connected platforms apply their own rules, permissions and availability. A connection shown in the dashboard does not guarantee that a provider will accept every publication or live stream.

Spiritual and emergency matters

Ministry content is spiritual support and Christian teaching. The platform is not an emergency service and does not replace qualified medical, legal, financial or emergency assistance where such professional help is needed.

Enforcement and preservation

We may restrict access, preserve relevant records, or cooperate with valid lawful process when reasonably necessary for safety, security, legal compliance or dispute resolution. We do not provide a hidden law-enforcement backdoor or grant access merely because someone claims official authority.

Changes

These terms may be updated as the platform changes. Material changes should be reflected by updating the date below and, where appropriate, presenting a renewed acknowledgement to affected users.
