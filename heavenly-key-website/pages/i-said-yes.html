I Said Yes to Jesus

Steps to Salvation

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life."

John 3:16
Step 1

Acknowledge

Admit that you have sinned and need God's help.

"For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God" (Romans 3:23)
"God be merciful to me a sinner" (Luke 18:13)

Step 2

Confess and Repent

Be genuinely sorry for the sins you have committed and ask God to forgive you.

"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness" (1 John 1:9)
"Except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish" (Luke 13:3)

Step 3

Forsake

Determine that with God's help you are going to turn away from all sin in your life.

"Let the wicked forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts: and let him return unto the LORD…for he will abundantly pardon" (Isaiah 55:7)

Step 4

Believe

When you have honestly and sincerely taken the steps above, then believe that God will hear your prayer and save you.

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life" (John 3:16)

Step 5

Receive

God will let you know that the work is done in your heart.

"The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God" (Romans 8:16)

Have You Prayed This Prayer?

"Lord Jesus, I acknowledge that I have sinned against You. I repent of my sins and turn away from them. I believe that You died for me and rose again. Please forgive me, cleanse me, and save me. I receive You as my Lord and Saviour. Write my name in the Book of Life. Thank You for saving me. Amen."

If you prayed this prayer, you are now saved!
