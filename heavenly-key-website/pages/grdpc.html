What is GRDPC?

Global Revival & Destiny Prayer Cry is a global prayer programme of Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement. It brings believers together across churches, ministries and nations to call upon God in faith, stand upon His Word and seek His intervention in spiritual life, family, destiny, ministry and the harvest of souls.

GRDPC is not a separate movement. It is part of Heavenly Key Evangelistic Movement's end-time burden to awaken believers, strengthen prayer, encourage holy living and prepare people for the coming of the Lord.
