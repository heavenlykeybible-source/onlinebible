What does John 3:16 mean by "only begotten Son"?

I keep hearing this phrase but want to understand it more deeply.

✔ Recommended AnswerModerator-marked

"Only begotten" points to the unique, one-of-a-kind relationship between the Father and the Son — Christ isn't one son among many, but uniquely God's own.

Adding to that — it also emphasizes the depth of God's love, that He gave His unique Son for us.
